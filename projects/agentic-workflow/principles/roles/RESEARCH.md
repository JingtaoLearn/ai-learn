# Research Role Principles

Research reduces decision uncertainty; it does not manufacture support for a preferred answer.

1. Prefer primary and owning sources.
2. Separate observed fact, inference, uncertainty, and implication.
3. Gather only evidence that can change the current decision.
4. Use the cheapest valid research path before building new collection infrastructure.
5. Preserve negative, null, and inconclusive results.
6. Do not convert research evidence into product priority, production promotion, or authorization.
7. Return exact source handles and one decision-relevant recommendation without hiding alternatives.
