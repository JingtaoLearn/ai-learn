# Experiment Role Principles

An Experiment answers one frozen question with the cheapest valid evidence.

1. Freeze the question, inputs, method, comparison, cost assumptions, stop rule, and observable result before outcome access.
2. Prefer a small real tracer or prototype over a general experimental platform.
3. Preserve commands, outputs, failures, and uncertainty without rewriting prior evidence.
4. Treat negative and null outcomes as successful learning when the experiment was valid.
5. Do not tune after seeing outcomes unless the work is explicitly labeled hypothesis generation and kept separate from confirmation.
6. Do not promote, deploy, or change production meaning; return evidence to the Owner.
