# Agentic Workflow Product Principles

These Principles apply to the Agentic Workflow product itself. Shared engineering judgment is defined in [`principles/DEFAULTS.md`](principles/DEFAULTS.md).

1. Real AI Agents are the functional and decision nodes; files and tools support their judgment rather than replacing it.
2. Goal, Principles, and Validation remain distinct authority sources.
3. Dynamic Action selection is required, but every choice is constrained by explicit Principles and evidence.
4. Skills are optional professional methods; Tools are bounded capabilities; neither becomes a fixed hidden pipeline.
5. Use native Hermes resources before building custom workflow infrastructure.
6. Preserve one canonical Owner Session per product and one Profile per stable Role.
7. Current delivery is functional prototype validation: show the intended capability to the user and observe actual effect before broad engineering.
8. A valid stop, negative result, or unsupported hypothesis is preferable to manufactured progress.
9. The Product Owner chooses a coherent outcome rather than mechanically decomposing everything into the smallest Action.
10. The Owner completes short, clear, low-risk and reversible work directly when delegation would cost more than it adds.
11. Delegation is justified only when specialist judgment, isolation, parallelism, durable recovery or independent review materially improves the outcome.
12. Maintain one executable workflow generation. Preserve superseded systems as audit evidence, not as parallel runtimes.
13. Keep the framework's source/version history separate from concrete runtime data. Product repositories and GitHub Issues/PRs remain normal development tools.
14. Provide non-blocking user understanding through Owner-accountable Outcome reports and product-group Threads; follow `REPORTING.md`.
15. Validate through a real product journey under `VALIDATION.md`; component canaries and a fixed observation period do not replace actual usefulness.

## Explicit Default overrides

None.
